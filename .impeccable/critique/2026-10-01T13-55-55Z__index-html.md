## Design Specificity Verdict

## Overall Impression

The bones are good: real local-business specificity, a working conversion path, zero technical errors. But the page currently ships with its three highest-trust moments empty (diferenciais, team, Google rating), a CTA repeated so often it cancels out its own salience, and a handful of execution-level "AI slop" tells (low-contrast eyebrow labels, a dark glow shadow, an overused font, a flat type scale) that keep it from reading as fully intentional. The single biggest opportunity: fix the trust gaps and tone down the CTA repetition before anything else — those two issues sit exactly at the moment an anxious first-time patient decides whether to message a stranger on WhatsApp.

## What's Working

1. **FAQ answers real fears, not generic questions** — "Colocar implante dói?" and "Clareamento dental estraga o dente?" mirror how a nervous patient actually searches, with plain, reassuring answers (`index.html` 606-640).
2. **The custom heartbeat-line divider** built from the clinic's own symbol SVG, with a looping CSS animation that respects `prefers-reduced-motion` — a bespoke transition device no generic template ships (`css/style.css` 262-292).
3. **Context-aware WhatsApp pre-fills** — every CTA pre-writes a message specific to the section it is in, a small real friction reduction a templated site would not bother with.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Empty trust-critical sections render as visibly broken UI, not "pending content"**
Why it matters: `.diferenciais-list li` items are empty but still fully styled as light-blue rounded pills (`css/style.css` 466-473) — three blank boxes, not an absent list. `.avaliacoes-resumo` renders two empty `<span>`s on a dark background. These are exactly the two moments an anxious first-time patient checks before messaging a stranger on WhatsApp ("why this clinic," "are other patients happy here") — and right now they look like a bug.
Fix: do not render empty styled containers — omit these elements from the DOM entirely until real content (diferenciais text, Google rating/count, team bios) is supplied. Visually, replace with nothing (not a skeleton) so the section around them still reads as finished.
Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`

**[P1] CTA repeated at identical visual weight 9+ times flattens hierarchy**
Why it matters: header, hero, identifica, procedimentos, educativo, convenios, processo, final, localizacao, and footer all carry the same green pill at the same salience, plus the always-visible floating WhatsApp button — nothing is permitted to be *the* moment. On mobile this means Casey (the primary stated audience) passes a WhatsApp-styled CTA almost every screen while the floating button is already pinned on-screen.
Fix: keep full "hero-weight" styling for 2-3 CTAs (hero, after social proof, final band) plus the floating button; down-style the mid-page section CTAs to a lighter secondary treatment.
Suggested command: `$impeccable quieter`

**[P1] Eyebrow labels fail WCAG AA contrast — detector-confirmed, pervasive**
Why it matters: every section's small uppercase label ("O QUE FAZEMOS," "A CLÍNICA," etc.) is set in `--azul-marca` (#3AB0FF) at ~12.5px against white/near-white backgrounds, landing at 2.1-2.4:1 — about half the 4.5:1 required. This is the one finding in this critique that is a hard accessibility bug, not a taste call, and it touches nearly every section since the eyebrow pattern repeats throughout.
Fix: darken the eyebrow color (e.g. toward `--azul-escuro` or a mid-tone that still reads as "brand blue" but clears 4.5:1 at that size) or increase size/weight enough to qualify as "large text" (3:1 threshold). This does not require abandoning light blue as the primary color — it is an execution fix within the existing palette.
Suggested command: `$impeccable audit` (to re-verify after fix) / fold into `$impeccable polish`

**[P2] Dead link in the social-proof section**
`index.html` line 551: `<a class="link-onlight" href="#">Ver mais no Google →</a>`. Why it matters: a visitor motivated enough to verify reviews directly on Google — natural trust-verification behavior for a health service — clicks through to nothing.
Fix: remove the link until the real Google Business Profile URL exists, or point it at a Google Maps search URL for the clinic name in the meantime.
Suggested command: `$impeccable harden`

**[P2] Execution-level "AI slop" tells, detector-confirmed**
Three markers the detector flags as characteristic of generic AI-generated UI, which directly conflicts with your explicit "não pode ter cara de feito por IA" requirement: (1) Plus Jakarta Sans as the only typeface — one of the handful of faces every AI-UI wave converges on; (2) a colored glow box-shadow (#0b4f7a) on a dark section — the detector's own description calls this "the default 'cool' look of AI-generated UIs"; (3) a flat type scale (body/h4/h3 within 1.10x of each other, versus a 1.25x target), reinforcing that the H1 being locked inside an SVG image (rather than real styled text) suppresses the kind of bold size jump that would read as intentional.
Fix: pick a more distinctive type pairing for headings, convert the H1 to real text at a genuinely larger size, remove the glow shadow in favor of neutral elevation.
Suggested command: `$impeccable typeset`

**[P3] Header logo click forces a full page reload instead of scroll-to-top**
`js/main.js` lines 11-16 sets `window.location.href` to the same path on logo click — a full re-fetch of HTML/CSS/JS/images just to return to the top, and the only navigation on the page that skips the smooth-scroll every other anchor gets.
Fix: scroll to `#topo` like every other in-page link.
Suggested command: `$impeccable polish`
